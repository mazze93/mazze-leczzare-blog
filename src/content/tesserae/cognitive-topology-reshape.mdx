The first version of cognitive-topology was built before I knew Stratum existed.
It reimplemented, in prose, three things Stratum had already shipped and
superseded: the tessera as an editable file, memory tiers as stores, and twelve
roles shipped at once. The reshape turns it into Stratum's first ingress client.
It emits explicit decisions as events and reads their status back as a fold.
The change is an integration, not a deletion.

This tile is the handoff for that reshape. Its human view is rendered from its
own machine record: thirteen genesis events, `ct-000` through `ct-012`, embedded
in the page. Drag the core-depth scrubber to replay the fold. `ct-011` loses its
evidence and the reframe un-ratifies as you move back past their markers.

## The records

- [ct-reshape@0.6.0 spec](/cognitive-topology/reshape/cognitive-topology-reshape.spec.md): the execution spec, ratified 2026-09-06. The envelope and birth-status legality are confirmed from Stratum source.

## Status

The reframe is ratified. It has not been executed yet. Marketplace `0.2.0` is
the target, and its completion will post as ledger events before `v0.2.0` is
tagged. Until those events exist, this tile records a decision, not a delivery.

The architecture this reshapes is on the [Cognitive Topology](/cognitive-topology/) page.

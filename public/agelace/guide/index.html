Setting up

About fifteen minutes, most of it waiting for downloads. You'll install one free program, unzip a folder, and double-click. You never need to type a command.

You needA Windows 10 or 11 PC, or a Mac. About 3 GB of free space. An internet connection for the first start only.

About the picturesagelace's own screens are real screenshots. Docker Desktop's screens are drawn, because they change between versions. Yours may look a little different, but the words will be the same.

  1. Get Docker Desktop

    Docker Desktop is a free program that lets agelace run in its own box on your computer, without installing anything else.

    Go to docker.com/products/docker-desktop and press the download button for your computer. On Windows it's Download for Windows.

  2. Install it, and say yes

    Open the file you downloaded and keep the ticked options as they are. Press OK, then Close.

    Windows may ask to restart, or to update WSL (a part of Windows that Docker uses). Say yes to both. That's normal.

    When Docker Desktop opens for the first time, accept its terms. You can skip signing in: agelace doesn't need a Docker account.

  3. Wait for “Engine running”

    Docker Desktop takes a minute to settle. Look at the bottom left of its window: when it says Engine running, it's ready.

    You can close the window after that. Docker keeps running quietly in the background, and starts by itself whenever your computer does.

  4. Download agelace

    Open agelace's releases. At the top is the newest; under its Assets, click the file that starts with agelace- and ends in .zip.

    It's tiny, a few KB. The app itself downloads in step 7.

  5. Unzip it somewhere you'll find again

    Move the zip to your Documents folder. Windows right-click it and choose Extract All…, then Extract. Mac double-click it.

    You now have a folder called agelace-0.2.0-beta.1. This folder is agelace. Everything you write will live inside it.

  6. Double-click start-agelace

    Open the new folder. Windows double-click start-agelace.cmd. Mac double-click start-agelace.command.

    The first time, Windows may warn you, because the file came from the internet. If it says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. If it says “Open File – Security Warning”, click Run. On a Mac, right-click it, choose Open, then Open again.

  7. Let it fetch, once

    A black window opens and says Fetching agelace…. The first time it downloads the app, which takes a minute or two. After that, starting takes seconds.

    Leave the window alone. When agelace is ready, your browser opens and the black window closes by itself. If it stops and says something went wrong, it waits for you to read it; see below.

  8. Your world opens

    Your web browser opens agelace by itself. If it doesn't, open your browser and go to http://localhost:8080.

    Bookmark that address. It only works on your computer, because agelace lives there and nowhere else.

    Name something in the box at the top left, choose what it is, and press Inscribe. You've begun.

  9. Your world is a folder

    Everything you make is saved as you type, into the world folder inside the agelace folder. There's no save button and no account.

    To back up: copy the whole world folder somewhere safe, such as a USB stick or cloud storage. That's the entire backup.

    Deleted things go to world/.trash, never away. Drop your own font files into world/fonts and they appear in the Forge within seconds.

  10. Stopping, and coming back

    To stop agelace, double-click stop-agelace (.cmd on Windows, .command on a Mac). Or just leave it running; it uses very little, and it comes back by itself whenever Docker Desktop starts.

    To come back later, open your bookmark. If it doesn't load, double-click start-agelace again.

    When a new version arrives: unzip it and copy its files over your agelace folder, keeping your world folder. Then start it as usual.

On a Mac

The steps are the same. Download Docker Desktop for Mac, choosing Apple chip or Intel to match your Mac (the Apple menu, then About This Mac, says which). Then use the .command launchers instead of .cmd.

The first time you open a launcher, macOS may say it's from an unidentified developer. Right-click it, choose Open, then Open again. It remembers after that.

On a tabletagelace can open on a tablet on the same Wi-Fi. READ-ME-FIRST.txt in the folder explains how. Only do it on a home network you trust.

If something goes wrong

It says “Docker isn't running”

Open Docker Desktop and wait for Engine running at the bottom left (plate 3). Then double-click start-agelace again.

Docker Desktop says virtualization is turned off

That's a setting in the PC's start-up (BIOS) menu, and the one step here that might need a helper. Ask Mazze; it takes five minutes and only happens once.

The black window says something went wrong

Open Docker Desktop, click Containers, click agelace, and open the Logs tab. Take a photo of it and send it to Mazze.

“agelace can't write to its world folder”

The folder is somewhere agelace isn't allowed to write, such as a read-only drive. Move the whole agelace folder into your Documents and start it again.

The browser says it can't connect

Wait ten seconds and refresh. The first start can be slow. If it still doesn't load, double-click start-agelace again.

“Port 8080 is already in use”

Another program is using that address. Ask Mazze; it's a one-line change.

This is a betaIt works and it's tested, but it hasn't met many computers yet. Anything confusing or broken is worth telling Mazze. That's the most useful thing a beta can get.

# Content licence

Two licences cover this repository.

- **Code** (everything that builds and runs the site): MIT, in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- **Essay text** in `src/content/blog/`: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), © Mazze LeCzzare. You may share and adapt it, including commercially, as long as you credit the author and link to the original post on mazzeleczzare.com.

## What CC BY does not cover

- **Posts that opt out.** A post with `license: "all-rights-reserved"` in its frontmatter keeps all rights. Its page shows no licence notice.
- **Images**, unless a post says otherwise.
- **Quoted material** by other people, which stays under its owners' terms.
- **Everything outside `src/content/blog/`**: other collections, standalone pages under `public/`, papers under `public/papers/`, and design assets.

## Why

Essays published here are archived with Rogue Scholar, which registers a DOI for each one and requires CC BY. The DOI resolves back to the post on this site. A post can stay CC BY and still be left out of archiving with `archive: false`.

A licence already granted for a published version can't be taken back for that version. Changing a post's licence later applies only from then on.
